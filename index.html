One parent company, three trades.

Holistic Ventures is a Dhaka-based sourcing and logistics company. We buy, move and clear goods across borders for ourselves and for partners.

Our work covers industrial and consumer electronics, bulk apparel and textiles, and the freight and customs paperwork that sits between a supplier and a shelf. We also run Silly Socks, our own consumer brand.

Our services

Four service lines, built around your cargo and your compliance requirements.

Electrical procurement

We source industrial components, heavy circuitry and consumer electronics, and track them from the factory to the final warehouse.

Apparel and textiles

Operating brand: Silly Socks

Bulk transit of knitwear and novelty consumer fashion, with large shipments handled as one managed movement.

Customs clearance

We prepare the documents and handle the filings so cargo moves from port to warehouse without avoidable delays.

Global freight

Air, sea and land transport coordinated door to door, with the route and schedule visible from pickup to delivery.
